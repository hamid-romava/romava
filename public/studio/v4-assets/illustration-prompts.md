## Theatre hero

Built-in image generation with the original refined icon sheet as the drawing-style
reference. A luxurious historic theatre from the horse-carriage era. Tall proscenium
arch, rust velvet curtains, dark wood stage and restrained Persian carpet detail.

spotlight illuminates the chair and an oval pool on the stage. The light symbolizes
Romava. Irregular navy ink contours, elongated chair legs and architectural proportions,

ivory #F6F0E6. Landscape composition with safe margins for existing hero crops.
